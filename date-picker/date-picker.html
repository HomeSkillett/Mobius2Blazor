Mobius DatePicker

All 40 visual states from DefaultControls.xaml 6794-7711. Full element map, resolved dimensions and citations in specs/DatePicker-SPEC.md. Hover and focus are live; states that need a WPF condition are simulated with .is-* classes.

Two things that render differently than the XAML reads. The popup has no border — Styles_Calendar sets BorderBrush (7688) and BorderThickness (7690) but the template never binds either, so both setters are dead. And the header row is 44px, not the declared 40 — MinHeight clamps it (2643, 2768). Full list of never-rendering setters at the top of date-picker.css.

ARIA note for developers. Day cells carry aria-label, aria-current="date" and aria-pressed, so selection and today are not conveyed by colour alone. A full role="grid" / role="row" / role="columnheader" tree would give screen readers row and column position, but needs six row wrappers this markup does not have. Left out rather than invented — worth a decision before Blazor handoff.

5. Focus Ring on Cells deviation from Master

XAML CalendarButtonFocusStates (6902) and ActiveStates (6906) are both declared empty. Day and month cells have no focus ring in Master, so tabbing into the grid shows nothing — a WCAG 2.4.7 gap. Adding .mob-calendar--a11y-focus to the popup opts into a ring. Off by default. Tab through the cells below to compare.

Master behaviour — no ring

Opt-in — 2px MouseOverPrimary ring

6. Popup Disabled

Whole-popup dim at opacity 0.35 (XAML 7347). The load fade is suppressed in this state, otherwise it would animate to full opacity before snapping back down.
