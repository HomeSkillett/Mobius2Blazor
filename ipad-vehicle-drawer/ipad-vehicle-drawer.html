iPad Vehicle Drawer (Exploration)

An exploration of the iPad vehicle drawer from Figma, assembled from library components: vertical SplitPanel, ListBox with the vehicle item, and the DisplayGaugePanel with the T8360 gauges. Figma is the reference here, so this page is not a pixel match to master.

Figma is drawn in the Dark theme. Pick Dark from the theme menu (gear, top right) to compare.

Open

Try it: tap a vehicle to select it, tap the tab chevron to hide or show the gauges, tap the handle to close the drawer.
